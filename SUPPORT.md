# Support

Thanks for your interest in this project! Here's how to get help.

## Before you ask

- Read the **[README](README.md)** — what the system is, the quick start
  (`docker compose up`), and the live dashboard tour.
- Dig into the docs: **[docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)** (design,
  coordination, split-brain writeup) and **[docs/BENCHMARKS.md](docs/BENCHMARKS.md)**.
- Search **[existing issues](https://github.com/kripa-sindhu-007/task-queue-educational-dashboard/issues?q=is%3Aissue)**
  — your question may already be answered.

## Getting help

- **Questions & ideas** — open a
  [Discussion](https://github.com/kripa-sindhu-007/task-queue-educational-dashboard/discussions)
  (if enabled) or a [feature request](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/feature_request.md).
- **Bugs** — open a [bug report](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.md) with steps
  to reproduce and your environment (OS, Docker version, Go version).
- **Security issues** — do **not** open a public issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Good to know

This is a solo-maintained, educational project, so responses are best-effort.
Clear, reproducible reports get resolved fastest. Contributions are very welcome —
see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
